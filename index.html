From a scan to a clearer memory.

1. Choose an image, drop it in the editor, or paste with Ctrl/Cmd + V. Try the sample if you want to explore.

2. Repair: paint a small damaged area, then Fill damage. Erase mask or Clear mask to correct your selection.

3. Enhance: choose the blur type and strength. Reduce noise, correct faded color, then upscale. Each button applies one edit.

4. Read text: select an area or enter its coordinates. Enhance text area changes those pixels. Read text with OCR also cleans and enlarges a private crop before recognition.

5. Compare with the slider. Zoom, undo, redo, or reset. Download PNG for lossless output or JPG for a smaller file.

Ctrl/Cmd + Z undo · Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z redo. Use Tab to navigate; arrow keys switch tool tabs and adjust sliders. Brush keyboard controls are in Repair.

Limits: 25 MB per file, 60 megapixels decoded, 1.5 megapixels working input, 6 megapixels output. Undo keeps at most 48 MB of pixel history. Closing this tab discards your work.

Private by design.

Your image is processed with Canvas and a local Web Worker. Depix does not send image pixels or detected text to a server, use analytics, or store your images.

Only OCR needs the internet: jsDelivr supplies open-source code and trained language data. Those providers receive normal connection metadata, such as your IP address, but not your image. OCR language files may be cached by your browser.

Use only images you own or have permission to edit. Keep your originals. OCR and restoration can be wrong. Do not treat enhanced text as proof or an accurate record.

This app uses classic restoration, not generative photo AI. Generative AI tools elsewhere can add details that were never in the original. No restoration can recover detail that is truly lost.

Depix is free software under the MIT license, provided as-is without warranty. PNG retains transparency. JPG flattens it onto white. Image metadata is not retained.
